Privacy policy — Airelay

Airelay is provided by Akhan Almagambetov. Contact: texnically@gmail.com. Effective date: 2026-11-15.

Airelay does not require an account. The app has no advertising or analytics SDKs, and the developer does not receive your transferred files or usage data through the app.

Files and folder information are stored in Airelay on your device. The app shares only files received into its own storage, not your photo library, external Files-provider documents, or other apps’ storage. Theme and language preferences are stored on the device.

When you enable sharing, paired browsers on your local network can upload, list, preview, download, and manage received files. HTTP traffic is not encrypted. Use a trusted network. The pairing code limits access; it does not encrypt traffic. A paired browser can access received files until sharing stops.

Turning sharing off, locking the device, or leaving the app stops sharing. Completed files remain until you delete them or remove the app. Incomplete transfers are discarded; remnants from an interrupted process are cleaned at the next launch. iOS backups may include completed files according to your device settings.

If you share a file through AirDrop or another app, the chosen service handles it under its own terms.

For privacy questions or support, contact texnically@gmail.com. This policy will be updated if Airelay’s data practices change.

If you choose to leave an optional one-time tip in a version that offers this feature, Apple processes the purchase through the App Store under its own terms and privacy policy. Airelay verifies transaction information on your device to acknowledge the tip. The app does not receive your payment-card details or Apple Account password. Tips do not unlock features, do not renew automatically, and are not required to use the app.

Where offered, monthly support is an optional auto-renewing subscription processed by Apple. The selected amount is charged monthly until you cancel through Apple’s subscription settings. Airelay checks verified subscription and renewal information on your device to show the current plan, renewal or end date, and scheduled changes, and offers restoration through Apple. Subscribing, cancelling, or restoring never changes access to file-transfer features; they remain free. Apple’s terms and privacy policy govern payments.

If you email support, I receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. I use them to answer your request and keep relevant correspondence as needed for support or legal obligations. You may request deletion by email, subject to those obligations. Please omit sensitive information.

This website uses no analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, or cookies set by me. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process technical request data, including IP addresses, for hosting and security under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Your email provider also processes support email under its own terms.
