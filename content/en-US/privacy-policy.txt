Privacy policy

Airelay is provided by {{OWNER_NAME}}. Contact: {{SUPPORT_CONTACT}}. Effective date: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}.

Airelay does not require an account. The app has no advertising or analytics SDKs, and the developer does not receive your transferred files or usage data through the app.

Files and folder information are stored in Airelay on your device. The app shares only files received into its own storage, not your photo library, external Files-provider documents, or other apps’ storage. Theme and language preferences are stored on the device.

When you enable sharing, paired browsers on your local network can upload, list, preview, download, and manage received files. HTTP traffic is not encrypted. Use a trusted network. The pairing code limits access; it does not encrypt traffic. A paired browser can access received files until sharing stops.

Turning sharing off, locking the device, or leaving the app stops sharing. Completed files remain until you delete them or remove the app. Incomplete transfers are discarded; remnants from an interrupted process are cleaned at the next launch. iOS backups may include completed files according to your device settings.

If you share a file through AirDrop or another app, the chosen service handles it under its own terms. If you contact support, you choose what information to send; do not include private files or pairing credentials. Information collected by a separately hosted support website must be disclosed by that website’s operator.

For privacy questions or support, contact {{SUPPORT_CONTACT}}. This draft must be updated if the app’s data practices change.
